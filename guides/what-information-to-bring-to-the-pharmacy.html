What information to bring to the pharmacy

By the RxSafeCheck team · Educational, reviewed for plain-language clarity · Morning Mind LLC

Your pharmacist can do a lot more for you when they have the full picture. Whether you're picking up a new prescription or asking a question, bring these.

Bring this every time

  • A complete medicine list — prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and supplements, with doses.
  • Allergies and past reactions to any medicine.
  • Your health conditions — especially kidney or liver problems, pregnancy, or breastfeeding.
  • Your questions, written down so you don't forget.

Helpful extras

  • Insurance or prescription discount card.
  • The name of your main doctor.
  • Any recent changes — a hospital stay, a new specialist, a new supplement.
Make it easy: Keep one printable medicine summary and hand it over each visit. RxSafeCheck builds this for you — with your medicines, possible interactions, and questions to ask — ready to print.

Questions worth asking

  1. Does this interact with anything else I take?
  2. How and when should I take it?
  3. What side effects should I call about?
  4. Is there a lower-cost option?
