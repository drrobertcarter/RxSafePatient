Why we exist

Our mission & vision

Our mission is to help patients, caregivers, and families better understand medication safety, treatment complexity, and the questions they should ask their healthcare teams. Through RxSafeCheck™, we provide clear, accessible information that supports safer conversations between patients, physicians, pharmacists, specialists, and caregivers. We do not replace medical advice, diagnose conditions, or recommend treatments — we help people become better prepared, better informed, and better equipped to take part in decisions about their care.

Our vision is a healthcare world where every patient has the knowledge, confidence, and support needed to ask the right questions about medications, diagnoses, treatment changes, side effects, drug interactions, and risks.

What's Safe Together?

Safe Together is the belief that safer healthcare happens when patients, caregivers, and medical teams communicate openly and work as one team.

RxSafeCheck was built by a physician and a biomedical scientist whose personal experience shaped this mission. After losing his father to prostate cancer — following more than nine years of changing diagnoses, new medications, new treatments, emergency room visits, and complex medication interactions — he saw firsthand how difficult it can be for families to navigate modern healthcare. His father was fortunate to have a son with experience in public health and physiology, and access to a network of healthcare providers and experts. Most patients and families do not have that same support.

That is why RxSafeCheck™ was created — not to tell patients what treatment to choose, but to help patients and caregivers understand what questions to ask their doctors, pharmacists, nurses, and specialists. It's a conversation tool, not a diagnosis or treatment tool, helping identify topics worth discussing: medication interactions, side effects, treatment changes, chronic drug exposure, acute reactions, and risks that deserve closer attention.

Because safety is not something patients should have to figure out alone. Safety happens when patients, caregivers, and medical teams are informed, prepared, and safe together.

Why this matters

Medication safety is one of healthcare's quietest, most serious problems

Adverse drug events — including drug interactions, side effects, and dosing errors — are among the most common preventable harms in medicine. Most are never dramatic; they build quietly, one prescription at a time. Understanding your own medicines, and asking the right questions, is one of the simplest ways to lower that risk.

2.7M+

Hospitalized patients in the U.S. experience an adverse drug event each year, by one widely cited estimate.

~80,000

U.S. drug-overdose deaths in 2024 — down about 27% from 2023, but still a leading cause of death for adults 18–44 (CDC).

~600,000

Deaths worldwide each year are attributed to psychoactive drug use, most involving opioids (WHO).

Beyond overdoses, some patient-safety advocates argue that adverse drug events, taken together, rank among the leading causes of death in the U.S. — a claim that is debated among researchers, but one that reflects a real and under-recognized burden. Older adults and people taking several medicines at once carry the highest risk, which is exactly where a clear medication list and good questions help most.

Sources: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (National Vital Statistics System, 2024 provisional overdose data); World Health Organization (opioid overdose fact sheet); American Society of Pharmacovigilance; peer-reviewed literature on adverse drug events. Figures are estimates from public sources and are provided for general awareness, not as medical or statistical advice. RxSafeCheck is educational and does not diagnose or treat.

Educational, honest, and private

We built RxSafeCheck to help you learn and start conversations — not to replace your healthcare team.

  • Not medical advice. RxSafeCheck is educational. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace your pharmacist or doctor.
  • Private by default. Your medication list stays in your browser for the session only — no account required, and we do not sell your data or use advertising trackers.
  • Grounded in public sources. Built on well-established pharmacology and public resources like the U.S. National Library of Medicine (RxNorm, DSLD) and ClinicalTrials.gov.
  • "Nothing found" isn't a guarantee. The knowledge base is curated, not exhaustive — always confirm with your pharmacist.

RxSafeTravel™ — plan safer trips with your conditions & medicines

Travel disrupts routines — time zones, missed doses, heat, altitude, long flights, new foods, and care away from home. For people managing chronic conditions or several medications, small disruptions can become real concerns. RxSafeTravel™ helps you get ready by organizing the health and medication information worth discussing with your care team before you go.

Answer a few questions about your trip, conditions, and medicines, and RxSafeTravel builds a personalized set of questions for your doctor, pharmacist, and travel-medicine clinician — plus pre-, during-, and post-travel checklists, an emergency card, and a printable plan. It's a conversation tool, not medical advice.
