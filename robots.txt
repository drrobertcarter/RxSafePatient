<div class="wrap"><section><div class="trust">
  <h2>Educational, honest, and private</h2>
  <p style="color:#C7DED9;max-width:680px">We built RxSafeCheck to help you learn and start conversations — not to replace your healthcare team.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Not medical advice.</strong> RxSafeCheck is educational. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace your pharmacist or doctor.</li>
    <li><strong>Private by default.</strong> Your medication list stays in your browser for the session only — no account required, and we do not sell your data or use advertising trackers.</li>
    <li><strong>Grounded in public sources.</strong> Built on well-established pharmacology and public resources like the U.S. National Library of Medicine (RxNorm, DSLD) and ClinicalTrials.gov.</li>
    <li><strong>"Nothing found" isn't a guarantee.</strong> The knowledge base is curated, not exhaustive — always confirm with your pharmacist.</li>
  </ul>
