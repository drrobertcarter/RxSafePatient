How to build and keep a medication list

By the RxSafeCheck team · Educational, reviewed for plain-language clarity · Morning Mind LLC

The single most useful thing you can do for medication safety is keep one accurate, current list — and show it everywhere. Here's how to build it.

What to include for each item

  • Name (brand and generic if you know both).
  • Strength and dose (e.g., "10 mg, 1 tablet").
  • How and when you take it.
  • What it's for.
  • The prescriber.

Don't forget these

  • Over-the-counter medicines (pain, allergy, sleep, antacids).
  • Vitamins and supplements.
  • Patches, inhalers, eye drops, creams, and injectables.
  • Allergies and past reactions.

Keep it current

  • Update it whenever something starts, stops, or changes.
  • Bring it to every appointment and pharmacy visit.
  • Keep a copy on your phone and one where family can find it.
